# Use Split View

Split View shows two to four tabs side by side in one window. The side you clicked last gets the address bar, the keyboard and page commands, and a border shows which one it is. A Split View stays together in the sidebar until you end it.

## See two tabs side by side with Split View {#split-view-create}

1. Drag a tab from the sidebar onto the page you're looking at.
2. Or right-click a tab in the sidebar and choose Split with Current Tab.
3. Click a side to choose which page the keyboard and address bar use.

Split With Next Tab splits the current tab with the one below it.

## Add a third or fourth tab to Split View {#split-view-add-tab}

1. Drag another tab from the sidebar onto the Split View.
2. Or right-click the tab and choose Split with Current Tab.
3. A Split View holds up to four tabs.

## Open a link beside the current page {#open-link-in-split-view}

1. Right-click a link on the page.
2. Choose Open Link in Split View.

## Switch between the sides of a Split View {#split-view-focus}

1. Click the side you want to use. Its border shows it has focus.
2. Or use Focus Next Split Card and Focus Previous Split Card from the Tabs menu.

Shortcut: **Control-Command-Right Arrow**

## Make Split View follow your pointer {#split-view-focus-follows-pointer}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Tabs.
2. Turn on Focus follows pointer.

## Make one side of a Split View wider {#split-view-resize}

Drag the line between the two pages left or right.

## Swap the sides of a Split View {#split-view-move-card}

1. Click the page you want to move.
2. Choose Tabs > Move Split Card Left or Move Split Card Right.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-Left Arrow**

## Take one tab out of a Split View {#split-view-remove-tab}

1. Click the page you want to take out.
2. Choose Tabs > Remove Tab From Split. The other tabs stay together.

## End a Split View {#split-view-separate}

1. Choose Tabs > Separate All Tabs. Every page becomes its own tab again.
2. To close all its tabs instead, right-click the Split View in the sidebar and choose Close Split.

Shortcut: **Option-Command-U**

## Name a Split View or change its color or icon {#split-view-rename}

1. Right-click the Split View's row in the sidebar.
2. Choose Rename Split View…, Change Icon… or Change Color.

A Split View can't be pinned and stays in its Space. Separate it first to pin its tabs or move them to another Space.
