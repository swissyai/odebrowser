# Tabs and folders

Ode's sidebar keeps the tabs you use every day apart from the ones you only need for now, so your open tabs can come and go without losing anything.

## What pinned, saved and open tabs are {#sidebar-sections}

Pinned tabs are the icons at the top, for sites you use every day. Saved tabs sit above the line and stay until you delete them. Open tabs sit below the line and move to Archive when you clear them or after a while.

## Close and bring back tabs

### Close a tab {#close-tab}

1. Press Command-W, or right-click the tab in the sidebar and choose Close Tab.
2. An open tab goes to Archive, so you can bring it back.
3. Closing a pinned or saved tab only puts its page away. The tab stays in the sidebar.

Shortcut: **Command-W**

### Reopen a tab you closed {#reopen-closed-tab}

1. Choose Tabs > Reopen Closed Tab.
2. Do it again to bring back the tab you closed before that.
3. For older tabs, open Archive and click the one you want.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-T**

### Archive a tab {#archive-tab}

1. Show the open tab you want to archive.
2. Choose Tabs > Archive Tab. The tab leaves the sidebar and waits in Archive.

Shortcut: **Command-E**

Archive Tab works on open tabs. Pinned and saved tabs stay in the sidebar.

### Clear all open tabs at once {#clear-open-tabs}

1. Choose Tabs > Clear Unpinned Tabs, or move the pointer over the sidebar and click Clear on the line above your open tabs.
2. Every open tab in this Space moves to Archive. Pinned and saved tabs, and other Spaces, stay.
3. To bring them all back in their order, choose Tabs > Reopen Closed Tab right after, or open Archive.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-K**

### Archive old open tabs now {#clean-up-old-tabs}

1. Click ••• beside the Space's name and choose Clean Up Current Tabs.
2. Open tabs you haven't used for longer than each Space's archive time move to Archive.

It works in every Space, and skips tabs that are showing or playing sound. To clear every open tab in this Space, choose Tabs > Clear Unpinned Tabs.

### Change when open tabs archive themselves {#auto-archive-time}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click your Space in the sidebar and click Browsing.
2. Under Archive current tabs, choose After 12 Hours, After 24 Hours, After 7 Days, After 30 Days or Never.
3. Click Archive Now to run it right away.

Each Space has its own setting. New Spaces archive after 12 hours.

### Sort open tabs into folders automatically {#tidy-open-tabs}

1. In Settings > Search > Tab organization, turn on Enable suggestions and pick a Tab organization model.
2. Move the pointer over the sidebar and click Tidy on the line.
3. Check the folders it suggests, rename any, then click Apply. Nothing changes until you do.

Tidy needs intelligence, which is off until you turn it on.

## Pinned and saved tabs

### Pin a site to the top of the sidebar {#pin-tab}

1. Right-click the tab in the sidebar and choose Pin Tab, or drag it to the top.
2. Pinned tabs show as icons at the top of the Space and keep the address you pinned.

Shortcut: **Command-D**

A Space holds up to 12 pinned tabs.

### Unpin a tab {#unpin-tab}

1. Right-click the pinned tab and choose Unpin Tab.
2. It moves down to your open tabs. Close it there if you don't need it.

Shortcut: **Command-D**

### Keep a tab in the sidebar {#save-tab}

1. Drag the tab above the line in the sidebar.
2. Or right-click it and choose Save in Folder > Saved Tabs.
3. Saved tabs stay until you delete them and keep the address you saved.

### Remove a saved tab {#remove-saved-tab}

1. Right-click the saved tab in the sidebar.
2. Choose Delete Tab to remove it, or Move to Current Tabs to make it an ordinary open tab.

### Go back to a pinned or saved tab's original page {#return-to-saved-url}

1. Double-click the pinned or saved tab in the sidebar.
2. Or right-click it and choose Edit Tab > Return to Saved URL.

### Change the page a pinned or saved tab keeps {#replace-saved-url}

1. Go to the page you want the tab to keep.
2. Right-click the tab and choose Edit Tab > Replace with Current URL.

### Choose where a pinned tab reopens after you close it {#saved-tab-after-closing}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Tabs.
2. Under Pinned and saved tabs, set After closing to Resume last location or Return to saved URL.
3. Turn on Click the favicon to return to the saved URL if you like.

## Folders

Folders work above the line, for saved tabs, and below it, for open tabs you're working through.

### Make a folder for tabs {#create-folder}

1. Click the ••• button beside the Space's name and choose New Folder, then type its name.
2. Or right-click an open tab and choose Add to Current Tabs Folder > New Folder.
3. Drag tabs onto the folder to file them.

### Put a folder inside another folder {#nested-folder}

1. Right-click a folder and choose New Nested Folder.
2. Or drag a folder onto another folder.

Folders can nest up to 16 levels deep.

### Move tabs into a folder {#move-tab-into-folder}

1. Drag the tab onto the folder in the sidebar.
2. Or right-click the tab and choose Save in Folder, or Add to Current Tabs Folder for an open tab.
3. To move several, select them first, then right-click and choose Move to Folder.

### Rename a folder {#rename-folder}

1. Right-click the folder and choose Rename Folder.
2. Type the new name and press Return.

### Change a folder's color or icon {#folder-color-icon}

1. Right-click the folder and choose Folder Color… or Folder Icon.
2. Pick a color, an emoji or a symbol.
3. To show only the icon, turn on Icon Only Folders in Settings > Appearance.

### Move a folder between saved and open tabs {#move-folder}

1. Right-click the folder.
2. Choose Move to Saved Tabs or Move to Current Tabs. Its tabs and nested folders go with it.
3. Or drag the folder across the line.

### Delete a folder {#delete-folder}

1. Right-click the folder and choose Delete Folder.
2. Confirm. Its tabs stay in the same section, and nested folders move up one level.

## Change how a tab looks

### Rename a tab {#rename-tab}

1. Right-click the tab in the sidebar.
2. Choose Rename Tab. For a pinned or saved tab, it's under Edit Tab.
3. Type the new name and press Return.

### Give pinned and saved tabs short, tidy titles {#tidy-tab-title}

1. In Settings > Search > Tab organization, turn on Enable suggestions and pick a Tab organization model. Tidy titles when pinning is then on.
2. To tidy one tab, right-click it and choose Edit Tab > Tidy Title.
3. To undo, choose Edit Tab > Restore Original Title.

These need intelligence, which is off until you turn it on.

### Change a tab's icon {#change-tab-icon}

1. Right-click the tab in the sidebar. For a pinned or saved tab, open Edit Tab.
2. Choose Change Icon… to pick an emoji or symbol, or Pull New Icon to fetch the site's icon again.

## Move around your tabs

### Move to the next or previous tab with the keyboard {#switch-tabs-keyboard}

1. Choose Tabs > Next Tab or Previous Tab.
2. To jump to a tab by its place, press Command and a number from 1 to 9.

Shortcut: **Option-Command-Down Arrow**

### Jump back to the tab you were just on {#most-recent-tab}

1. Choose Tabs > Most Recent Tab.
2. Do it again to flip back.

Shortcut: **Control-Tab**

It jumps straight to the tab. There's no tab switcher panel.

### Move a tab up or down the sidebar {#reorder-tabs}

1. Drag the tab up or down in the sidebar.
2. Drop it above the line to save it, or into a folder to file it.

### Select several tabs at once {#select-several-tabs}

1. Command-click tabs in the sidebar to pick them, or Shift-click to pick a range.
2. Right-click one of them to pin, move, split, duplicate, archive or delete them together.

### Duplicate a tab {#duplicate-tab}

1. Right-click the tab in the sidebar and choose Duplicate Tab.
2. Or choose Tabs > Duplicate Tab.

## Nest tabs in a tree

With nesting on, the sidebar shows which tab each link came from, indenting each level a little further. Ode's exports keep your trees.

### Nest tabs opened from a page under it {#tree-tabs}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Layout, turn on Nest tabs opened from a page.
3. Links you open in a new tab now appear under the tab they came from.

Only open tabs nest. Closing a tab moves the tabs under it up a level instead of closing them, and tabs on top or in the icon sidebar show without nesting.

### Nest a tab under another, or move it out {#nest-tab}

1. Right-click an open tab and choose Nest Under Tab Above.
2. To take it out again, right-click it and choose Move Out a Level.
3. Or drag a tab onto the middle of another open tab to nest it there.

These need Nest tabs opened from a page turned on in Settings > Appearance > Layout. With it off, dropping a tab on another makes a folder.

### Hide the tabs nested under a tab {#collapse-nested-tabs}

1. Move the pointer over a tab that has tabs under it and click the arrow beside its close button.
2. Or right-click the tab and choose Hide Nested Tabs. The number beside it shows how many are hidden.
3. Click the number, or choose Show Nested Tabs, to see them again.

Next Tab and Previous Tab skip hidden tabs. Closing a collapsed tab doesn't close the tabs under it.

## Sound, sleep and memory

### Mute a tab that's playing sound {#mute-tab}

1. Look for the speaker mark on a tab in the sidebar.
2. Click it to mute the tab. Click it again to unmute.

The player at the bottom of the sidebar also has a Mute button.

### Change when tabs go to sleep {#sleeping-tabs}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Sleeping tabs, set Put tabs to sleep after to 5, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 or 3 hours, or Never.

The default is 15 minutes. The five tabs you looked at most recently never sleep, and a tab in a Space that no window shows sleeps after half the time. Tabs that are showing, playing sound, in a call, using the camera or microphone, holding text you typed, or kept loaded never sleep either.

### Keep a tab from going to sleep {#keep-tab-loaded}

1. Right-click the tab in the sidebar.
2. Choose Keep Loaded. Choose Stop Keeping Loaded to undo it.

Ode may still unload it if your Mac runs very low on memory.

### Put a tab to sleep to free memory {#unload-tab}

1. Right-click the tab in the sidebar and choose Unload Tab.
2. The tab stays in the sidebar and loads again when you click it.

Middle-clicking a pinned tab also unloads it.
