# Sidebar, windows and appearance

## The sidebar

From top to bottom, the sidebar has your pinned tabs, the Space's name, saved tabs, the line, and open tabs. At the bottom are the player for whatever is playing, recent downloads and the Space icons.

### Hide or show the sidebar {#hide-sidebar}

1. Choose View > Toggle Sidebar. The sidebar slides away and the page fills the window.
2. While it's hidden, move the pointer to the window's edge. The sidebar comes out over the page until you move away.
3. To keep it shown again, choose Toggle Sidebar or click the sidebar button at the bottom of the sidebar.

Shortcut: **Command-S**

With tabs on top, Command-S hides the bar of tabs, and the top edge brings it back.

### Stop the hidden sidebar from coming out at the window's edge {#sidebar-at-window-edge}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Window, turn off Show sidebar at window edge.

It's on by default. With it off, click the window's edge or press Command-S to bring the sidebar back.

### Change how easily a hidden sidebar slides out {#sidebar-hover-area}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Window, drag the Sidebar hover area slider. Smaller means you have to reach closer to the edge.

The slider works while Show sidebar at window edge is on.

### Put the sidebar on the right {#sidebar-on-right}

1. Choose View > Move Sidebar to the Right.
2. To move it back, choose View > Move Sidebar to the Left.

It's also Sidebar on right in Settings > Appearance > Layout. It isn't available while tabs are on top.

### Make the sidebar wider or narrower {#resize-sidebar}

1. Move the pointer to the sidebar's edge beside the page.
2. Drag it left or right.

Double-click the edge to go back to the standard width. Dragging it far in shows the sidebar as icons, then hides it.

### Show the sidebar as icons {#icon-only-sidebar}

1. Choose View > Show Sidebar as Icons, or drag the sidebar's edge in until it turns into icons.
2. Rest the pointer on an icon to see its title.
3. To see titles again, choose View > Show Full Sidebar or drag the edge out.

Shortcut: **Control-Command-S**

It's also Sidebar in Settings > Appearance > Layout. Dragging the edge narrower than 150 points switches to icons, and narrower than 60 points hides the sidebar. Icons aren't available with tabs on top.

### Where History, Archive and Downloads are {#common-lists}

Click the list button at the bottom of the sidebar, beside the Space icons. Choose Archive, History or Downloads. Each shows only the current Space.

### Control music and video from the sidebar {#now-playing}

1. Play sound or video in a tab, then switch to another tab. The player appears at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Use its buttons to play, pause, skip or mute.
3. To turn the player off, go to Settings > Tabs > Sidebar widgets and turn off Now Playing.

The keyboard's play-pause key controls it too. The player hides while its tab is on screen.

### Go back to the tab that's playing {#back-to-playing-tab}

1. Click the player's artwork or title at the bottom of the sidebar, or its Back to Tab button.
2. Or choose Tabs > Back to Playing Tab from any window.

Shortcut: **Option-Command-Return**

Ode finds the tab in whatever Space or window it's in. A locked Space asks you to unlock it first.

## Tabs on top

### Show tabs across the top of the window {#top-tabs}

1. Choose View > Show Tabs on Top.
2. The bar holds the Space's menu, pinned tabs as icons, then saved and open tabs.
3. To go back, choose View > Show Tabs in Sidebar.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-S**

It's also Tabs in Settings > Appearance > Layout. Folders, History and Downloads open the sidebar over the page.

With tabs on top, each tab has a close button and Pin, Duplicate and Close in its menu. To reorder tabs, use the sidebar over the page.

## How Ode looks

Appearance settings apply to every Space on this Mac and don't sync.

### Use light or dark mode {#light-dark-appearance}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Window, set Appearance to System, Light or Dark.

To darken a website itself, use a Boost.

### Choose the color of selected tabs and the address bar {#accent-color}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Turn off Follow Space accent to pick your own color. Leave it on to use each Space's color.

### Fit more tabs with Compact density {#layout-density}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Layout, set Density to Compact. Rows, pinned tabs and the address field get shorter.

Comfortable is the default. To make text and icons smaller too, change Tab scale.

### Make tabs bigger, smaller or rounder {#tab-size-and-shape}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Tabs and pins, set Tab scale to Small, Default or Large, and change the corner radius and pin layout. The preview shows the result.

Reset All… at the bottom puts every appearance choice back.

## Windows

### Open a new window {#new-window}

1. Choose File > New Window.
2. Both windows show the same Spaces and tabs. Each keeps its own selected Space and tab.

Shortcut: **Command-N**

### Open a temporary window that keeps nothing {#new-blank-window}

1. Choose File > New Blank Window.
2. It uses the current Space's sign-ins and passwords, but its tabs and history go away when you close it.

Shortcut: **Option-Shift-Command-N**

For browsing without your sign-ins, use a private window.

### Close a window {#close-window}

1. Choose File > Close Window.
2. Your tabs stay in the sidebar of your other windows and the next time you open Ode.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-W**

### What happens when you drag a tab out of the window {#drag-tab-out}

Dragging a tab outside the window moves it into a temporary window of its own. Drag it back into a normal window to keep it. Its page closes with the temporary window.

### Use Ode in full screen {#full-screen}

1. Choose View > Enter Full Screen.
2. Choose View > Exit Full Screen to leave.

Shortcut: **Control-Command-F**
