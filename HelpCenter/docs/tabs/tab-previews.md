# Tab previews for Gmail and Google Calendar

## See new mail and your next meeting from a pinned tab {#tab-previews}

1. Pin or save Gmail or Google Calendar and open it once.
2. Its icon shows your unread count, or how soon your next meeting starts.
3. Rest the pointer on the tab to see recent mail or upcoming events.

Previews read from the tab's own page and stay on this Mac.

The badge shows up to 99+ unread messages. For Google Calendar it counts down to a meeting that starts within the hour, and says Now while one is on. Click a message or an event in the preview to open it in that tab.

## Where previews come from

Ode reads a preview from the tab's own page, with the sign-in that tab already has. Nothing goes through a server of Ode's, and previews are kept in memory only: not on disk, not in exports and not in sync. Private windows never show previews, and agents can't read them.

## Turn tab previews off {#tab-previews-off}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Tabs.
2. Under Tab previews, turn off Show previews of pinned and saved tabs, or just Gmail or Google Calendar.
3. You can also turn off Tab Previews in Site Controls on those sites.

## Why a tab preview is empty or out of date {#tab-preview-not-showing}

Previews read from the tab itself, so open the pinned tab once after Ode starts. A sleeping tab keeps its last preview. Choose Keep Loaded on the tab to keep it fresh. If it says you're signed out, sign in to the site in that tab.

Previews work for Gmail and Google Calendar, in pinned and saved tabs.

## Previews for Outlook, Notion and other sites {#outlook-previews}

Tab previews work for Gmail and Google Calendar only. Other sites, such as Outlook, show no preview yet.
