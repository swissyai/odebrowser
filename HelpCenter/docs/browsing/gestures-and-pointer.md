# Gestures, clicks and drags

Many things in Ode happen with a drag or a click instead of a button.

## Swipe

- Swipe left or right with two fingers over the sidebar to change Spaces.
- Swipe left or right on a page to go back or forward.

## Drag

- Drag a tab onto the page to open it beside the page in Split View.
- Drag tabs and folders up and down the sidebar to reorder them. Drop a tab above the line to save it, or onto a folder to file it.
- Drag a tab onto a Space's icon at the bottom of the sidebar to move it there.
- Drag a tab out of the window to move it into a temporary window.
- Drag a link off a page to preview it in Peek. Hold Option to drag the link itself.
- Drag a finished download into Finder or another app.
- Drag the line between Split View pages to resize them, and the sidebar's edge to resize the sidebar.

## Click

- Right-click a tab, folder, link, Space or download for everything you can do with it.
- Option-click a link to open it in Peek. You can change this to Command-click in Settings > Links.
- Command-click or Shift-click tabs to select several at once.
- Double-click a pinned or saved tab to go back to the address it keeps.
- Double-click an empty part of the sidebar to open a new tab.
- Middle-click an open tab to close it. Middle-click a pinned or saved tab to put its page to sleep.
- Right-click the back arrow to see the pages before.

## Hover

- Rest the pointer on a pinned Gmail or Google Calendar tab to see its preview.
- Move the pointer to the window's edge to see a hidden sidebar.
- Rest the pointer on a finished download and press the Space bar to preview it.
