# Boosts

A Boost changes how one website looks to you: darker, warmer, in another font, or without the parts you don't want. Ode applies it every time you visit that site in the Space where you made it.

## Restyle a website with a Boost {#boost-site}

1. Open the site and choose Page > Boost This Site.
2. Use the Boost bar over the page: Colors, Font, Zap to hide things, or CSS.
3. Click Done. Ode saves it and applies it every time you visit the site in this Space.

Boosts work on Chromium and WebKit pages, one per site in each Space.

## Make a website dark or change its colors {#boost-colors}

1. Choose Page > Boost This Site, then click Colors.
2. Turn on Dark, pick a Tint, or move the Brightness and Contrast sliders.
3. Click Reset Colors to start over, then Done.

Tint strength goes from 10% to 100%. Brightness and Contrast go from 50% to 150%, where 100% leaves the site as it is.

## Change a website's font {#boost-font}

1. Choose Page > Boost This Site.
2. Open Font and pick one, such as New York, Georgia or Rounded.
3. Choose Site's Font to go back to the original.

Code and icons keep the site's own font.

## Hide parts of a website {#boost-zap}

1. Choose Page > Boost This Site, then click Zap.
2. Click each thing on the page you want to hide. Press Escape to stop.
3. Click Done. Those parts stay hidden on that site.

## Bring back something you hid with Zap {#boost-unhide}

1. Choose Page > Boost This Site.
2. Click the Hidden button in the Boost bar.
3. Choose one item to show it again, or Show All.

## Add your own CSS to a website {#boost-css}

1. Choose Page > Boost This Site, then click CSS.
2. Type your CSS and click Apply.

## Find and edit a Boost you made {#edit-boost}

1. Go to the site the Boost is for.
2. Click Site Controls at the right of the address, then click Edit beside Boost.
3. Or choose Page > Boost This Site.

There is no list of every Boost yet. Each one lives with its site.

## Turn a Boost off for a while {#boost-turn-off}

1. Go to the site and click Site Controls at the right of the address.
2. Turn off the switch beside Boost. Turn it on again whenever you like.

## Delete a Boost {#remove-boost}

1. Choose Page > Boost This Site.
2. Click the ••• button in the Boost bar and choose Remove Boost.

## Where Boosts apply and whether they sync {#boosts-scope}

A Boost belongs to one site in one Space. Make it again in another Space to use it there. Boosts stay on this Mac. They're saved in Ode's export but don't sync.

A Space keeps up to 500 Boosts.
