# Peek, Quick Window and links

Peek and Quick Window let you look at a link without adding a tab. Both use a Space's sign-ins and passwords, so the page works as it would in a tab.

## Peek

### Preview a link without leaving the page {#peek}

1. Option-click a link, or right-click it and choose Open Link in Peek.
2. Read it in the Peek over your page.
3. Press Escape to close it, or click Open In… to keep it as a tab.

Peek shows only for the tab you opened it from.

### Choose when links open in Peek {#peek-settings}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Links.
2. Under Peek, turn Open cross-site links from pinned and saved tabs in Peek on or off.
3. Set Open Peek with to Option or Command. The other key opens a new tab.

Open Peek with can also be Shift. Command-click then opens a new tab, or Option-click when Command opens Peek.

### Drag a link to preview it {#drag-link-to-peek}

1. Drag a link a little way off the page. A live preview follows the pointer.
2. Let go to keep it open in Peek.
3. Hold Option while you drag to move the link itself instead.

Turn this off with Drag links to Peek in Settings > Links.

### Change Peek's size and the page behind it {#peek-size}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Links.
2. Under Peek, set Peek size, and click Test Peek to try it.
3. Turn off Dim the page behind Peek to keep the page at full brightness.

## Quick Window

### Open a small Quick Window {#quick-window}

1. Choose File > New Quick Window, then type an address or search.
2. To keep the page, click Open in and pick a Space.
3. Close the Quick Window to drop it. It waits in Archive for a while.

A Quick Window has a browser window's controls: Back and Forward, Site Controls and the Space's pinned extensions, with the ones that don't fit behind ». Click its address or press Command-L to go somewhere else in the same Quick Window. Right-click a link and choose Open Link in New Quick Window to open it in another one.

The Quick Window shortcut also works from any app while Ode is open, and opens the Quick Window over the window you're in. Change or clear it in Settings > Shortcuts.

Shortcut: **Option-Command-N**

Option-Command-N opens it, as it opened Little Arc.

### Choose where links from other apps open {#links-from-other-apps}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Links.
2. Under Links from other apps, set Open in to Quick Window, Most Recent Space or Chosen Space.

Link routing rules run first.

### Choose how long Quick Windows are kept {#quick-window-cleanup}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Links.
2. Under Quick Window, set Auto-archive to 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or Never.
3. Closed Quick Windows are in Archive under Quick Windows.

### Why a small window opened when I signed in {#sign-in-popup}

When a site opens a pop-up, such as Sign in with Google, Ode shows it in a Quick Window in the same Space. It closes by itself when you finish signing in.

## Other ways to open links

### Open a link in another Space {#open-link-in-another-space}

1. Right-click the link on the page.
2. Choose Open Link in Another Space, then pick the Space.

To send links from other apps to a particular Space every time, see [Route links to the right Space](../link-routing/).
