# Pages: find, zoom, translate, save and share

Page commands act on the page you're looking at. In Split View, click the side you mean first.

## Read and find

### Find a word on the page {#find-in-page}

1. Choose Page > Find in Page and type what you're looking for.
2. Use the arrows to go to the next or previous match.
3. Click Close Find or press Escape when you're done.

Shortcut: **Command-F**

Chromium pages show how many matches there are. WebKit pages show only whether there's a match.

### Make a page bigger or smaller {#zoom-page}

1. Choose Page > Zoom In or Zoom Out.
2. Choose Page > Actual Size to go back to normal.

Shortcut: **Command-+**

### Set a zoom level for every page {#default-zoom}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Appearance.
2. Under Page, pick a Default page zoom from 50% to 300%.

### Read an article without clutter {#reader-mode}

1. Open the article.
2. Choose Page > Show Reader. Choose Hide Reader to go back.

Reader works on WebKit pages. On a Chromium page, choose Page > Open Page in WebKit first.

### Translate a page {#translate-page}

1. Choose Page > Translate Page.
2. To pick the languages, choose View > Show Translation Toolbar.
3. Choose Show Original in the toolbar to undo it.

Translation runs on your Mac with Apple's languages. Macs may need to download a language first.

### Translate pages automatically {#auto-translate}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click General.
2. Under Translation, turn on Translate automatically and choose the languages.
3. Turn off Offer to translate pages if you don't want to be asked.

### Search the web for selected text {#search-selected-text}

1. Select the text on the page.
2. Right-click it and choose Search with and your search engine's name.

## Move around

### Go back or forward a page {#back-forward}

1. Click the back or forward arrow at the top of the sidebar.
2. Or swipe left or right with two fingers on the page.

Shortcut: **Command-[**

Right-click the back arrow to see earlier pages.

### Reload a page {#reload-page}

1. Choose Navigate > Reload Page.
2. If it still looks wrong, choose Navigate > Reload from Origin to skip saved copies.
3. Choose Navigate > Stop Loading to stop.

Shortcut: **Command-R**

## Copy, share and save

### Copy the link to the current page {#copy-page-link}

1. Choose Page > Copy Page Link.
2. Paste it anywhere.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-C**

### Copy a page link as Markdown {#copy-link-markdown}

1. Choose Page > Copy Page Link as Markdown.
2. Paste it into notes or a document. It has the page's title and address.

Shortcut: **Option-Shift-Command-C**

### Share a page {#share-page}

1. Choose Page > Share.
2. Pick how to share it, such as AirDrop, Messages or Mail.

### Take a screenshot of part of a page {#capture-page}

1. Click Site Controls at the right of the address, then click Capture.
2. Drag a rectangle over the part you want. It's copied, ready to paste.
3. For the whole page, use Copy Full Page Capture in the developer toolbar.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-2**

### Print a page {#print-page}

1. Choose File > Print.
2. Pick your printer and click Print.

Shortcut: **Command-P**

### Save a page as a PDF {#export-pdf}

1. Choose Page > Export as PDF.
2. Choose where to save it and click Save.

### Save a whole page to read offline {#save-web-archive}

1. Choose Page > Save Web Archive.
2. Choose where to save it. Open it later with File > Open File.

WebKit pages save a .webarchive file and Chromium pages an .mhtml file. Each engine opens only its own kind.

### Open a file from your Mac {#open-file}

1. Choose File > Open File.
2. Pick the file. It opens as a tab in the current Space.

## Video

### Keep a video playing in a small floating window {#picture-in-picture}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click General, and turn on Picture in Picture when switching tabs.
2. Play a video and switch to another tab. The video keeps playing in a small window.

It's on by default. A video that plays muted by itself stays on its page unless you started it, and some sites don't allow it.

## Site Controls

### What's in Site Controls {#site-controls}

Click Site Controls, the slider button at the right of the address. It has quick buttons, Boost, the page's engine, content blocking, security and the site's permissions.

For what's in each part, see [Content blocking and site permissions](../content-blocking-and-site-permissions/) and [Boosts](../boosts/).
