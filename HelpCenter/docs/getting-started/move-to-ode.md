# Move to Ode from Arc or another browser

Ode shows you everything it will bring before it changes anything, so you choose what comes over and which Space it goes to.

## Move everything over from Arc {#import-from-arc}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click Advanced, then click Import from Another Browser… and pick Arc. New users see this during setup.
2. Review each Space. Click anything to leave it out, and choose where it goes.
3. Click Import. Ode brings your Spaces, tabs, folders, archive, history, passwords and sign-ins.

Spaces that share one Arc or Zen profile arrive as one [Sub-Space](../sub-spaces/) group, so they keep sharing sign-ins. The review outlines each group and can bring a Space in on its own. If one Space of a group goes into a Space you already have, the rest of the group become Sub-Spaces of that Space's group. Imported passwords are saved once for each group.

macOS asks once to let Ode read Arc's saved passwords.

## Where my Arc favorites, pinned tabs and Today tabs went {#arc-import-mapping}

Arc's favorites became pinned tabs at the top. Past 12, the rest went into a saved folder called Imported Pinned Tabs. Arc's pinned tabs became saved tabs, in the same folders. Today tabs became open tabs, and Arc's archive is in Archive.

## What each browser brings

| Browser | What comes over |
| --- | --- |
| Arc | Spaces (each Arc profile's as one Sub-Space group), tabs, folders, colors, icons, archive, history, passwords and sign-ins |
| Zen | Spaces (each profile's as one Sub-Space group), Essentials, pinned tabs, folders, open tabs, colors and history |
| Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera, Dia, Comet, Aside, Chromium and other Chromium browsers | Profiles, bookmarks, open tabs, history and passwords |
| Safari | Bookmarks, windows, open tabs and history |
| Firefox | Windows, open tabs, pinned tabs, bookmarks and history |

History imports are limited to 500,000 distinct web addresses and 4,000,000 visit times per profile. The review lists capacity limits and history-read problems separately from unsupported or invalid addresses; successfully read data remains included. Ode brings visit times the browser still holds within those limits, not visits the browser has already removed. Imported history follows the destination Space’s history-retention setting.

A pending review keeps its read data until you import it, cancel it or close the import window. If Ode cannot hold another read for review, it refuses the new read rather than replacing a pending one. Check your imported history before deleting the old browser’s data.

## Import from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Zen or another browser {#import-from-other-browser}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click Advanced, then click Import from Another Browser.
2. Pick the browser. If it isn't listed, click Scan for more.
3. Review what comes over, then click Import.

Import from Another Browser… opens the same review over your current Spaces. A Space goes into the Space you already have with the same name, unless you choose another destination.

Passwords come from Chromium browsers such as Chrome, Brave and Edge. Safari, Firefox and Zen passwords stay there.

If your browser isn't found, choose Request a browser during setup to write an import request in Ode’s feedback composer. You can also [email a browser request](mailto:feedback@odebrowser.com?subject=Ode%20browser%20import%20request); this link opens your email app directly.

## Why I'm signed out of sites after importing {#import-stay-signed-in}

Sign-ins come over only from Arc, and only on pages that open in Chromium. From other browsers, sign in to each site once in the right Space. Ode Passwords can fill your imported passwords as you sign in.

## Bring your extensions from Arc or Chrome {#import-extensions}

The import review lists each Space's Chrome Web Store extensions. Turn off any you don't want. After the import, Ode offers to install the rest in their Spaces.

## After you import

Open each new Space once before you delete anything from your old browser. Check that you're signed in where you expect, that your folders look right, and that the extensions you rely on work.

To learn Arc's names for things in Ode, see [Coming from Arc](../coming-from-arc/).
