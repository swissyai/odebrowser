# Install, set up and update Ode

## Install Ode {#install-ode}

1. Download Ode for Mac from odebrowser.com.
2. Open the disk image and drag Ode into Applications, then open it from there.

Ode needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.1 or later.

If macOS says the app can't be verified, don't open it anyway. Download Ode again from odebrowser.com.

## Ode on Intel Macs, older macOS, Windows or Linux {#intel-and-older-macs}

Ode needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26.1 or later. There's no version for Intel Macs, older macOS, Windows or Linux.

## Make Ode your default browser {#make-default-browser}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click General.
2. Beside Default browser, click Make Default.
3. Confirm in the question macOS shows.

## Learn everyday moves with Getting Started {#getting-started}

1. Choose Help > Getting Started with Ode.
2. Try the seven everyday moves: the Command Bar, searching a site, Split View, Peek, switching Spaces, Boosts and asking an assistant.
3. Then practice tabs, folders and Split View in an example Space. Your own tabs don't change.

Show Me starts a move for you where it can. Ode checks off each move when you make it anywhere, and remembers where you stopped.

The seven moves use the same steps as these sections: [the Command Bar](../command-palette/#open-command-bar), [searching a site](../command-palette/#search-a-site), [Split View](../split-view/#split-view-create), [Peek](../peek-and-quick-window/#peek), [switching Spaces](../spaces-and-isolation/#switch-space), [Boosts](../boosts/#boost-site) and [asking an assistant](../command-palette/#ask-ai-from-command-bar).

## Run setup again {#rerun-setup}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Advanced.
2. Click Review & Customize Setup… to change your Spaces, or Rerun onboarding to start from the welcome screen.

## Updates

### Update Ode {#update-ode}

1. Ode downloads updates on its own. When one is ready, the sidebar shows Restart to update.
2. Click Restart to update, or keep working and the update installs when you quit Ode.

Automatic updates are on unless you turn them off in Settings > About. To check right away, choose Ode > Check for Updates.

Ode checks open pages for unsaved work before it restarts to update. Your Spaces and tabs aren't stored inside the app, so updating never touches them.

### Go back to an earlier version {#go-back-version}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click About.
2. Click Go back to an earlier version…, choose a version, click Download, then click Go back.

Ode checks that the earlier version is signed and notarized like yours, then reopens in it. Your Spaces, tabs and settings stay as they are, and Ode tells you first if the earlier version can't open something this one saved.

### See what's new in Ode {#whats-new}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click About.
2. Open What's New in Ode to read the latest changes.

The first time Ode opens after an update, the sidebar lists what changed. All changes shows the full list.

### Send feedback or report a problem {#send-feedback}

Type **bug**, **report bug**, or **feedback** in the Command Bar and choose **Report a problem**. You can also use Help > Share Feedback… on Mac, or Share Feedback in Settings > About on Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

1. Write your report and review the screenshot.
2. Choose Send to Ode, or Copy Text to use another app.

**Retake** captures the same Ode window; **Remove** leaves it out. Automatic screenshots are skipped in private browsing. App details are optional and include only the version, build, and OS shown in the form.

Only the report, attached screenshot, and selected app details go to Ode. A failed send keeps your draft. **Retry** tries it again. No browsing history or logs are collected. Ode clears a received report only if you have not edited it while sending. Unsent drafts stay available until you quit.

**Copy Text** copies the recipient and full report text. **Save** in the Screenshot section exports the image for webmail or another app. The optional **Open Email Link** under About feedback includes text only and leaves the screenshot in your Ode draft.

Feedback links on this help site open email directly; they do not open Ode’s native composer.
