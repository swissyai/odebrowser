# Coming from Arc

Ode works the way Arc did in most places: Spaces down the side, pinned tabs that keep their address, a Command Bar, Split View, Peek, Boosts and tabs that tidy themselves. A few things have new names, and a few aren't here yet.

## Arc's names for things in Ode {#arc-words}

Favorites are pinned tabs, Arc's pinned tabs are saved tabs, and Today tabs are open tabs. Little Arc is Quick Window, and Air Traffic Control is link routing. The Library's Archive, History and Downloads are behind the list button at the bottom of the sidebar.

## What Ode doesn't have yet

### Easels {#arc-easels}

Ode doesn't have Easels yet, and Arc's Easels don't come over in the import. To clip part of a page, use Capture in Site Controls and paste it where you keep notes.

### Notes {#arc-notes}

Ode doesn't have notes yet, and Arc's notes don't come over in the import. Copy Page Link as Markdown is handy for saving a page to the notes app you use.

### Live Folders {#arc-live-folders}

Ode doesn't have Live Folders. Pinned Gmail and Google Calendar tabs can show what's new when you hover over them.

### Arc Max, Ask on Page and AI previews {#arc-max}

Ode can't summarize or chat about a page, and doesn't rename downloads. With intelligence on, it can tidy tab titles and suggest folders for open tabs. To ask an AI something, type chatgpt or claude in the Command Bar and press Tab.

### Bring over Boosts from Arc {#arc-boosts-import}

Arc's Boosts don't come over in the import. Make them again with Page > Boost This Site. Colors, fonts, Zap and CSS work much as they did in Arc.

### The same pinned tabs in every Space {#shared-pins}

Each Space has its own pinned tabs, because each Space has its own sign-ins. Pin the site in each Space where you want it.

### A tab switcher you hold Control-Tab for {#tab-switcher-panel}

Ode has no tab switcher panel. Control-Tab jumps straight to the tab you used last. To pick from your recent tabs, open the Command Bar: Recent Tabs show before you type.

Sync between Macs and an iPhone app aren't available yet either. See [Sync and iPhone](../sync-across-devices/).
