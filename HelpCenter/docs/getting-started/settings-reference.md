# Settings reference

## Open Settings {#open-settings}

1. Choose Ode > Settings.
2. Use Search settings at the top of its sidebar to find a setting by name.

Shortcut: **Command-,**

You can also type a setting's name in the Command Bar.

The Settings sidebar lists General, Tabs, Search, Appearance, Links, Engines, Shortcuts, Sync, Privacy & Permissions and Ad Blocking, then each of your Spaces, then Agents, Advanced and About. Settings for the whole app apply to every Space on this Mac. A Space's own pages apply only to it.

## General

Default browser and Make Default. Startup: what happens When Ode opens, and the Default Space. Translation: Offer to translate pages and Translate automatically. Webpages: Check spelling and Picture in Picture when switching tabs. Developer: Developer toolbar on local pages.

## Tabs

New tabs: whether tabs opened from links come to the front, and Follow tabs moved to another Space. Pinned and saved tabs: After closing, and Click the favicon to return to the saved URL. Tab previews for Gmail and Google Calendar. Split View: Focus follows pointer. Show recent tabs while switching, for when you hold Control-Tab. Sidebar widgets: Now Playing.

## Search

Default search, the search engine for Private windows, Search suggestions, Tab to search providers and Space to search providers. Results: Sections or Blended, Top Hit, Prefer open tabs and Show match reasons. Sections: the order of each kind of result. Also show: Settings Pages, @ Filters, Calculator, Paste and Go, Answers, Conversions, Dictionary and Web Answers, which is off until you turn it on. Typing: Complete addresses inline, Learn from my choices and Clear Learned Choices…. On Mac, Tab organization controls optional folder suggestions and title cleanup through Enable suggestions, with a Tab organization model choice and availability. The separate Ode group controls private on-device Answers in Command-T. Then every search engine, AI website shortcut and website, with Add Provider… and Restore Defaults…. AI Websites switches add Command-T shortcuts; they do not connect accounts or turn on Tab organization or Answers in Command-T.

## Appearance

Layout: Tabs (Sidebar or Top), Sidebar (Full or Icons), Sidebar on right, Density (Comfortable or Compact) and Nest tabs opened from a page. Window: Appearance, Animate pages when switching Spaces, Show sidebar at window edge, Sidebar hover area, the window border, Material and Follow Space accent. Page: Default page zoom. Tabs and pins: Tab scale (Small, Default or Large), corner radius, pin layout, colors and Dim unloaded tabs. Address field, Folders, Sleeping tabs and, for Chromium pages, Content blocking. Reset All… puts everything back.

## Links

Links from other apps, Routing, Quick Window and Peek, with Drag links to Peek, Dim the page behind Peek and Peek size. See [Peek, Quick Window and links](../peek-and-quick-window/) and [Route links to the right Space](../link-routing/).

## Engines

Default engine, with Use Recommended, and Website rules. See [Chromium and WebKit](../engines/).

## Shortcuts

Every command with its keys. Click a shortcut to record new keys; use its menu for Clear Shortcut or Reset to Default, and for Prefer Ode, Use Both or Prefer Website when a website uses the same keys. Reset All… resets everything. See [Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Sync

Sync with iCloud and its status. Sync isn't available in Ode yet; see [Sync and iPhone](../sync-across-devices/).

## Privacy & Permissions

System permissions: whether macOS lets Ode use the Camera, Microphone, Location, Notifications, Passkeys and Files & Folders. Below that, each Space's privacy page.

## Ad Blocking

Off, Balanced or Strict, the filter lists with Update Now, Your Lists, My Filters and Sites with Blocking Off. These settings stay on this Mac. See [Privacy, ad blocking and site permissions](../content-blocking-and-site-permissions/).

## Your Spaces

Click a Space to see its pages:

- Appearance: name, color and icon.
- Browsing: Search with, Search suggestions, Archive current tabs and Archive Now, Downloads, and deleting the Space.
- Privacy: Lock, Keep (how long history, Archive and download records are kept), Site permissions, Website data and Clear browsing data.
- Passwords: Ode Passwords, Sync with iCloud Keychain, Import Passwords… and Export Passwords….
- Extensions: Open Chrome Web Store, Chromium Extension Manager, Shortcuts… and the installed extensions.

Click + beside the Spaces heading to add one, or the pencil to reorder them. Right-click a Space for New Sub-Space, Make Sub-Space of and Separate from its primary. [Sub-Spaces](../sub-spaces/) show under their primary, and a group's Privacy and Passwords pages are shared by all its Spaces.

## Agents

Allow agents to use Ode, Connect an Agent, Add to a Config File, the Spaces agents may use, Let agents run scripts in pages, Connected and Allowed agents, and Activity. See [Let coding agents use Ode](../coding-agents/).

## Advanced

Setup: Rerun onboarding, Review & Customize Setup… and Import from Another Browser…. Feature Flags. Import and export: Import Browser Data…, Export Browser Data… and Export Bookmarks as HTML…. Automatic export. See [Backups and recovery](../backups-and-recovery/).

## About

Ode's version, Software updates, Crash and hang reports, What's New in Ode, and Feedback and support. Updates download and install on their own unless you turn that off, and Go back to an earlier version… reinstalls a recent version. See [Install and update Ode for Mac](../install-and-update-mac/).
