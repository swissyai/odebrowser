# Clear website data

Website data belongs to the Space that stored it and stays on this device. Clearing it in one Space never touches another Space, your saved passwords, extensions, downloaded files or site permissions. The Spaces of a [Sub-Space](../sub-spaces/) group share their website data, so clearing it in one clears it for the whole group.

## Clear one site {#clear-one-site}

1. Click Site Controls beside the address.
2. Choose Clear Cookies or Clear Cache.

Clear Cookies removes the site's cookies and everything else it stored in the Space, including local storage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage and service workers, and signs you out of the site. Clear Cache removes only what Ode kept to load the site faster.

Ode clears the whole site, such as example.com and its subdomains, in both engines, then reloads the page. You can also type Clear Cookies for This Site or Clear Cache for This Site in the Command Bar. In a private window, they clear only what that window holds.

## See and remove a Space's website data {#space-website-data}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space, then click Privacy.
2. Under Website data, choose Remove beside a site, or Remove All… for every site in the Space.

## Clear browsing data {#clear-browsing-data}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space, then click Privacy.
2. Under Clear browsing data, choose a time range, from Last Hour to All Time.
3. Choose History, Cookies and Site Data, or Cached Images and Files, then click Clear….

Clearing history in a Space that syncs removes it from your other devices too.
