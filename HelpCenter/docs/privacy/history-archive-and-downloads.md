# History and Archive

History lists the pages you visited. Archive holds the tabs you closed or that archived themselves. Both belong to one Space. The Spaces of a [Sub-Space](../sub-spaces/) group share one history.

## History

### See your browsing history {#show-history}

1. Choose View > Show History.
2. Type to search, or open the filter menu to show Today, Past Week or Past Month.
3. Click a page to open it again.

Shortcut: **Command-Y**

Each Space has its own history. Choose Time or Site above the list to group pages by when you last visited them or by their host. A leading `www.` is grouped with the same host without it.

Right-click a History row to open, pin or save the page. Pin and Save keep it in the current Space without opening it or changing the page you're viewing.

### Find a page you visited before {#search-history}

1. Open the Command Bar and type part of the page's title or address.
2. Type @history and a space first to see only history.
3. Or choose View > Show History and use its search field.

Shortcut: **Command-T**

History search matches titles and addresses, not the words on the page.

### Remove one page from your history {#delete-history-entry}

1. Open the Command Bar and type until the page shows.
2. Move down to it with the arrow keys and press Shift-Delete.

You can also right-click a row in History and choose Remove from History. Remove All from This Site removes that host and its subdomains, including visits outside the visible list, from this Space only. Saved and pinned tabs stay in place.

### Clear your history {#clear-history}

1. Choose View > Show History.
2. Open the filter menu and choose Clear History, then confirm.

Shortcut: **Command-Y**

This clears the current Space only. Other Spaces keep their history.

## Archive

### Find tabs that were closed or archived {#show-archive}

1. Choose View > Show Archive, or click the list button at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Archive.
2. Search or filter by Closed, Automatically Cleaned or Quick Windows.
3. Click a tab to bring it back.

To find archived tabs from the Command Bar too, turn on Archived Tabs under Sections in Settings > Search.

## How long things are kept

### Choose how long history and Archive are kept {#history-retention}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space, then click Privacy.
2. Under Keep, choose how long to keep History, Archived Tabs and Download Records: from 1 Day to Forever.

Shortening it deletes older records at once, so Ode asks first.

The Spaces of a Sub-Space group share these choices.

Downloads are on their own page: see [Downloads](../downloads/).
