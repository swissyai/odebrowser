# Downloads

Each Space has its own downloads list and its own download folder.

## See your downloads {#show-downloads}

1. Choose View > Show Downloads.
2. Or click the list button at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Downloads.
3. Each Space shows only its own downloads.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-J**

## The downloads at the bottom of the sidebar {#downloads-sidebar-foot}

Running and just-finished downloads show above the Space icons, up to three at a time. Click Hide to put away the finished ones, or Downloads to see them all. A finished file stays there for an hour.

## Drag a download into another app {#drag-download}

1. Show your downloads, or find the file at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Drag the finished file into Finder, Mail, Messages or a page's upload box.

## Open, preview or delete a downloaded file {#download-actions}

1. Right-click a finished download in the list.
2. Choose Open, Show in Finder, Quick Look or Copy.
3. Choose Move to Trash to delete the file, or Remove from List to keep the file and hide the row.

Rest the pointer on a finished download and press the Space bar to preview it.

A file you moved or deleted in Finder shows Moved or deleted, and only Remove from List.

## Pause, resume or cancel a download {#pause-cancel-download}

1. Right-click the download that's running.
2. Choose Pause Download, Resume Download or Cancel Download.

Pause and resume depend on the page's engine and the site.

## Clear the downloads list {#clear-downloads}

1. Show your downloads and click Filter Downloads.
2. Choose Clear Downloads. The files stay on your Mac.

## Choose where downloads are saved {#download-folder}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space, then click Browsing.
2. Under Downloads, click Choose Folder…, or turn on Ask where to save each download.

Each Space has its own download folder.

## Why a download was blocked {#download-blocked}

Ode blocks a site from downloading several files on its own, and warns about files that came over an insecure connection. If you trust it, right-click the download and choose Allow Download. To let a site download without asking, change Automatic Downloads in its Site Controls.

## Downloads and safety

Ode's Chromium engine has no Google services, so it can't check sites and files against Google's list of known dangerous ones. It still warns about files that can run software or came over an insecure connection. A finished download isn't a sign that a file is safe.

## What the downloads list keeps after you quit {#downloads-after-restart}

Each Space keeps its finished, canceled and failed downloads when you quit and open Ode again. A download older than the Space's Download Records setting in Settings > Privacy leaves the list. Clear Downloads empties it at once. Downloads in a private window are never kept. Your files stay in your download folder either way. The list stays on this Mac: it isn't synced or exported.

## Why Ode asks before quitting {#quit-with-downloads}

Ode asks when a download is still running, because quitting cancels it. Click Keep Browsing to let it finish, or Quit to cancel it.
