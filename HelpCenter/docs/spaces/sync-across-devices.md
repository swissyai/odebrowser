# Sync and iPhone

## Sync tabs between Macs {#sync-between-macs}

Sync isn't available in Ode yet. It's waiting for Apple to approve Ode's iCloud access. To move your setup to another Mac, use Export Now and Import Browser Data… in Settings > Advanced.

## Ode on iPhone and iPad {#iphone-app}

Ode for iPhone and iPad isn't available yet. Ode runs on Mac only.
