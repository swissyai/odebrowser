# Spaces

## What a Space is {#what-are-spaces}

A Space is a set of tabs with its own sign-ins, history, passwords and extensions. Use one for work and one for personal browsing, and switch between them in one window. A site signed in to in one Space isn't signed in to in another.

## What each Space keeps to itself

- Sign-ins, cookies and other website data. Chromium and WebKit each keep their own, so signing in on one engine doesn't sign you in on the other.
- Pinned, saved and open tabs, folders and Archive.
- History and downloads.
- Ode Passwords, site permissions and content blocking choices.
- Extensions and their settings.
- Its name, color and icon.

To let several Spaces share sign-ins, history, passwords and extensions while each keeps its own tabs and look, make them [Sub-Spaces](../sub-spaces/) of one Space.

## Use two accounts on the same site {#two-accounts-same-site}

Make a Space for each account. Sign in to the site once in each Space. Each Space keeps its own sign-ins. Switch Spaces to switch accounts.

## Switch to another Space {#switch-space}

1. Press Control-1 to Control-9 to jump to a Space by its place, as in Arc.
2. Or click the Space's icon at the bottom of the sidebar.
3. Or swipe left or right with two fingers over the sidebar.

Shortcut: **Option-Command-Right Arrow**

Option-Command-Right Arrow and Option-Command-Left Arrow move to the next or previous Space. Control-Right Arrow and Control-Left Arrow do too, once macOS stops using them for Mission Control.

## Switch Spaces with the keyboard {#switch-space-keys}

1. Press Control-1 to Control-9 to go to a Space by its place in the sidebar, as in Arc.
2. Press Option-Command-Right Arrow or Option-Command-Left Arrow for the next or previous Space.

Shortcut: **Control-1**

Control-Right Arrow and Control-Left Arrow switch Spaces too, once you turn off Move left a space and Move right a space in System Settings > Keyboard > Keyboard Shortcuts > Mission Control.

## Move a tab to another Space {#move-tab-to-space}

1. Right-click the tab and choose Move to Space, then pick the Space.
2. Or drag the tab onto the Space's icon at the bottom of the sidebar.

The tab opens with the other Space's sign-ins, not the ones it had.

## Stay put or follow a tab when you move it to another Space {#follow-moved-tabs}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click Tabs.
2. Turn Follow tabs moved to another Space on or off.

## Delete a Space {#delete-space}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space in the sidebar, then click Browsing.
2. Click the Delete button with the Space's name at the bottom, then confirm with Delete Space and Data.

This removes the Space's tabs, history, passwords, sign-ins and extensions. Downloaded files stay. You can't delete your last Space.

If you only want fewer tabs, clear or archive them instead. Deleting a Space can't be undone.

In a Sub-Space group, deleting a Sub-Space deletes only its tabs, and deleting the primary first asks which Sub-Space keeps the group. See [Delete a Space in a group](../sub-spaces/#delete).
