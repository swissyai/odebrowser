# Lock a Space or browse privately

There are two different kinds of privacy in Ode. Locking a Space hides it from anyone using your Mac. A private window keeps nothing after you close it.

## Lock a Space with Touch ID {#lock-space}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space, then click Privacy.
2. Under Lock, turn on Require Touch ID or password.
3. The Space locks again whenever Ode goes to the background.

A locked Space keeps its tabs and history. It only asks who you are before it shows them. Locking any Space of a [Sub-Space](../sub-spaces/) group locks the whole group.

While a Space is locked, its tabs, saved passwords and settings stay hidden, and agents see only its name.

## Browse privately {#private-window}

1. Choose File > New Private Window.
2. Ode keeps no history from it, uses no saved passwords, and forgets sign-ins when you close it.
3. Close the window to end private browsing.

Shortcut: **Shift-Command-N**

Private windows search with DuckDuckGo unless you change it in Settings > Search. Websites and your network can still see what you do.

Private browsing doesn't hide what you do from websites, your network or your employer. Files you download stay on your Mac.
