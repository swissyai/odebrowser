# Make and customize Spaces

A clear name and color for each Space tells you where you are before you sign in to anything.

## Make a new Space {#create-space}

1. Right-click an empty part of the sidebar and choose New Space.
2. Give it a name, a color and an icon.
3. Or choose Ode > Settings and click + beside the Spaces heading.

You can have up to 64 Spaces.

## Change a Space's name, color or icon {#space-name-color-icon}

1. Right-click an empty part of the sidebar and choose Edit Space.
2. On the Appearance page, type a name, click a color and pick an icon: a symbol, an emoji or a letter.

## Change the order of your Spaces {#reorder-spaces}

1. Choose Ode > Settings.
2. Click the pencil beside the Spaces heading, drag the Spaces into order, then click the checkmark.
3. Or right-click a Space there and choose Move Up or Move Down.

The order sets which Space Control-1 to Control-9 open.

## Choose the Space Ode opens in {#default-space}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, then click General.
2. Under Startup, choose the Default Space and what happens When Ode opens.

## Give a Space its own search engine {#space-search-engine}

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click the Space, then click Browsing.
2. Pick an engine under Search with.

## A Space's settings

Choose Ode > Settings and click a Space in the sidebar. Its pages are:

- Appearance: its name, color and icon.
- Browsing: its search engine, when open tabs archive themselves, its download folder, and deleting the Space.
- Privacy: locking it, how long it keeps history and Archive, site permissions and website data.
- Passwords: its saved passwords and password settings.
- Extensions: the extensions installed in it.

To make a Space that shares this one's sign-ins, right-click it in the Settings sidebar and choose New Sub-Space. See [Sub-Spaces](../sub-spaces/).
