# Share sign-ins across Spaces with Sub-Spaces

Every Space starts with sign-ins of its own. A Sub-Space uses another Space's sign-ins instead, while keeping its own tabs, folders and look. Use Sub-Spaces when several Spaces should be signed in to the same accounts, such as a Space for each client on one work account.

A Space and its Sub-Spaces form a group. The Space that leads the group is its primary.

## What a group shares {#what-a-group-shares}

The Spaces of a group share:

- Sign-ins, cookies, caches and other website data
- History
- Ode Passwords
- Extensions
- Site permissions and content blocking choices
- How long History, Archived Tabs and Download Records are kept
- The lock, and whether coding agents may use it

Each Space keeps its own:

- Open, saved and archived tabs, folders and Split View groups
- Name, color, icon and sidebar
- Browsing settings, such as its search engine and download folder

A setting the group shares says so at the end of its page, for example "Shared by Work's Sub-Spaces."

## Pinned tabs in a group {#pinned-tabs}

The Spaces of a group show their primary's pinned tabs, and a pinned tab opens the same page in each.

1. Choose Ode > Settings, click any Space in the group, then click Browsing.
2. Turn on Separate Pinned Tabs to give each Space pinned tabs of its own. The switch applies to the whole group.

## Make a Sub-Space {#make-sub-space}

1. Choose Ode > Settings.
2. Right-click a Space in the list, or click … on its page, then choose New Sub-Space.

You can also click + after the icons on a group's page, or right-click an empty part of the sidebar. The new Sub-Space copies the Space's look and settings and starts with one Start Page tab.

## Add a Space to a group {#join-group}

1. Choose Ode > Settings.
2. Right-click the Space in the list, or click … on its page, then choose Make Sub-Space of and pick the group.

You can also drag the Space onto another Space in the Settings list.

Ode asks first. The Space keeps its tabs and starts using the group's sign-ins, history and extensions. Its own sign-ins and website data stay on this device, unused, and come back if you separate it.

A Space that leads Sub-Spaces can't join another group, and private windows can't have Sub-Spaces.

## Switch between Sub-Spaces {#switch}

Sub-Spaces switch like any other Space. A group's Spaces sit together, primary first, and the Space picker outlines each group. Control-1 to Control-9 open Spaces by position, counting Sub-Spaces.

To move a whole group, drag its primary. Drag a Sub-Space among its group to reorder it.

## Separate a Sub-Space {#separate}

1. Choose Ode > Settings.
2. Right-click the Sub-Space in the list, or click … on its page, then choose Separate from the primary. You can also drag it out of its group.

The Space keeps its tabs and goes back to its own sign-ins, website data and extensions. History it recorded while in the group stays with the group.

A primary can't be separated. To end a group, separate or delete each of its Sub-Spaces.

## Delete a Space in a group {#delete}

Deleting a Sub-Space deletes its tabs. The group keeps the sign-ins, history and passwords it shares.

Deleting a primary asks which Sub-Space keeps the group, with its sign-ins, history, passwords and pinned tabs. To delete everything, choose to delete the primary and its Sub-Spaces. Unlock a locked group before you delete its primary.

## Open a page under another account {#open-in-space}

Right-click a link and choose a Space from Open Link in. The page opens in a Quick Window with that Space's sign-ins. Open This Page in, in Site Controls, does the same for the page you're on.

## Older versions of Ode on your other devices {#older-devices}

Devices with an earlier version of Ode keep syncing. They show each Sub-Space as a Space of its own, signed out, until they update. The first group you make reminds you to update Ode on your other devices.

## Groups from Arc and Zen {#import-groups}

Importing from Arc brings the Spaces of each Arc profile in as one group, led by the profile's first Space. Importing from Zen does the same for each profile's workspaces. Zen workspaces that share a container come in as a group of their own.

If the review sends one Space of a group into a Space you already have, the rest of the group become Sub-Spaces of that Space's group. Imported passwords go to the group's primary. The review outlines each group; choose Separate to bring a Space in on its own. See [Move to Ode](../move-to-ode/).
